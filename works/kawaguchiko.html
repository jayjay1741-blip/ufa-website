河口湖の山荘

山梨県 ／ 2023

森にとけこむ家 / A House Dissolved in the Forest

山梨県・富士山麓の静かな森に佇むこの山荘は、都心から車で約2時間という距離にありながら、鳥のさえずりに包まれた特別な環境にある。別荘地特有の隣接住宅からの視線を避けながら、建物が森そのものに溶け込むような設計を目指した。

クライアントは都心に暮らす家族。世代を超えた3家族が集い、それぞれのペースで過ごせる場所を求めていた。南斜面に富士山を望む敷地は東側に深い谷を抱き、そこから見上げる山並みは、深い森に迷い込んだような感覚を生み出す。

南面には大きな開口を設け、四季で移ろう森の表情を室内に取り込みながら、植栽と配置の工夫で周囲の視線を遮った。資材調達の困難を乗り越え、家族自らも施工に加わりながら完成させたこの空間は、当初の理想を超えるかけがえのない居場所となった。

Nestled in a quiet forest at the foot of Mt. Fuji in Yamanashi, this mountain lodge sits about two hours by car from central Tokyo, yet within a world of birdsong and stillness. To avoid the gaze of neighboring vacation homes, the design lets the building dissolve into the forest itself.

The clients are a Tokyo-based family spanning three generations, seeking a place where everyone could gather and still keep their own pace. Set on a south-facing slope with a view toward Mt. Fuji, the site holds a deep valley to the east — looking out from it feels like wandering into the depths of the forest.

Large openings on the south face draw in the forest's changing seasons, while careful planting and massing shield the surroundings from view. Built through a difficult timber shortage, with the family itself taking part in construction, the house became something more than what was first imagined — a place truly its own.
